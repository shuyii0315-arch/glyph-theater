Get the skill

A skill for Claude and other AI agents that turns classic ASCII characters into tiny stories. Give it a topic: it picks a character, writes four beats with real tension, and plays them as a looping scene in black and white, with one accent color.

UsesAgent skillASCII libraryscript engineself-check

Hand it off to AI

The prompt works in any AI tool, no install needed: paste it, answer two quick questions, and you get a looping story page. The skill goes further. It adds the character library, a fixed set of moves and a self-check, so every story comes out with the same rhythm and look.


  

   
Give this prompt to your agent: it installs the skill, and can update it later. In the Claude app, upload the .zip in your skill settings instead.
